Natural Stone Selection Guide

Practical questions to help you plan your marble or granite purchase.

Choosing a natural stone involves more than matching a color to a room. The right selection depends on the location, expected use, finish, installation method, care requirements and the characteristics of the specific material.

1. Choosing Stone for Flooring

  • Consider foot traffic, moisture, cleaning routine and the surface finish.
  • Ask the supplier and installer about slip resistance for the actual location.
  • Check the stone under lighting similar to the final space.
  • Confirm slab/tile dimensions, thickness, shade variation and installation method.

2. Choosing Stone for Kitchen Surfaces

  • Ask about staining, scratching, heat, sealing and maintenance for the exact stone.
  • Discuss edge profiles, cut-outs, support and installation tolerances with your fabricator.
  • Confirm whether the quoted price includes cutting, polishing, transport and fitting.

3. Stone for Stairs and Feature Areas

  • Discuss thickness, edge details, nosing, surface finish and safe footing.
  • Confirm measurements and installation specifications with the contractor.
  • Request a written scope describing material supply and any additional work.

4. Understanding Natural Variation

Natural stone can vary between slabs and batches. Veining, color, grain, texture and small natural characteristics may differ from online images or small samples. Review the actual material and clarify the selection process before confirming an order.

5. Estimating Quantity

Measure the project carefully and have the installer or supplier calculate material quantity and a suitable allowance for cuts, breakage and pattern matching. Do not rely on a generic online estimate for a final order.

6. Questions to Ask Before Paying

  • What is the exact material name, origin or grade, if applicable?
  • What are the dimensions, thickness and finish?
  • What is included and excluded in the quotation?
  • What are the delivery timeline, payment terms and return/cancellation conditions?
  • What cleaning products and maintenance practices are recommended?
This guide is general educational information, not a technical certification or substitute for advice from the stone supplier, fabricator and qualified installer for your specific application.
