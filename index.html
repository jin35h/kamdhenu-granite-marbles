Choose Stone with the Right Questions

Stone appearance is only one part of a successful selection. Ask about intended application, finish, thickness, shade variation, installation, maintenance and total project quantity.

  • Share your project location and intended use
  • Confirm dimensions and wastage allowance with your installer
  • Review actual samples or current stock where possible
  • Request written details for price, delivery and installation scope
